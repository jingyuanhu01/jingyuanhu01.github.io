Projects

Research interests

01

Geometry of single-cell data

I am interested in learning the manifold structure of single-cell gene-expression data and using differential geometry to understand cellular dynamics. This work includes FlowMap, a geometry-preserving method for embedding and interpreting RNA velocity.

02

Scaling laws in single-cell data

I am interested in how increasing dataset size relates to the amount of biological information that can be recovered. Using tools from random matrix theory, we develop scaling laws that characterize the behavior of representation learning in single-cell data.

03

Biological foundation models

I am interested in representation learning in biological foundation models. In particular, I study whether these models encode regulatory elements and their interactions within their learned embedding spaces.

I’m enthusiastic about learning new things—especially mathematics—and I enjoy sharing that process with others. Modern computational biology draws on many branches of math, often intersecting with genomics in unexpected ways. I see mathematics as a practical set of tools, and I taught myself much of the math I use through YouTube. That experience showed me how freely shared knowledge can help anyone build a deep understanding of difficult ideas.
